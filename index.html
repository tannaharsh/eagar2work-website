    Questions

    What people ask before they hire

    Straight answers about staff augmentation, remote developer costs, time zones and who owns the work.

    What is IT staff augmentation?

    It means adding outside engineers to your own team for as long as you need them. They work inside your process and tools like any other team member, while we handle recruiting, contracts, payroll and equipment. You get the extra hands without opening a full time position.

    How much does it cost to hire a remote developer?

    It depends on the role, seniority and how many hours you need. A dedicated remote developer usually costs a fraction of a US salary once you add benefits, recruiting fees and equipment. Tell us the role and we'll send a monthly rate the same week.

    Will the developers work in my time zone?

    Yes. Every engineer overlaps with your US business day for daily standups, meetings and quick questions. Tell us whether you're on Eastern, Central, Mountain or Pacific time and we'll set schedules around it.

    Who owns the code and the work they produce?

    You do. Every engineer signs an NDA, and our contract assigns all code, designs and documents to your company. Work lives in your repositories and your accounts from day one.

    How fast can someone start?

    For common roles like React, Node.js, Python or QA, you'll usually see profiles within a few days and can have someone working within one to two weeks after you pick them.

    What if a developer isn't the right fit?

    Tell us and we'll replace them at no extra cost, and help with the handover so your project doesn't lose ground.

    Can I reduce the team later?

    Yes. Engagements run month to month after the first month. Give us short notice and we'll scale the team down without penalties.
